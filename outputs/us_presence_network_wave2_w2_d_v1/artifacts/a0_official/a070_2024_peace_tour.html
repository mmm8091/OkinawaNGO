2024 VFP Okinawa/Japan Peace Speaking Tour Report -1-

August 08, 2024

Professor Yoshimi Tsuboi points to deadly levels of PFAS contamination flowing from the Mitsubishi Heavy Industry's Plant at the Komaki Airport near Nagoya, Japan. The airfield is used jointly by US Forces Japan and Self Defense Forces, Japan. 

Members of the VFP Peace Speaking Tour met with Dr. Tsuboi at a community center near Komaki Airport on August 7th.

The U.S. military is known to poison the air, soil, groundwater, and surface water at its bases throughout Japan with a host of deadly contaminants. Little attention, however, is given to contamination caused by hometown actors like Mitsubishi and the Japanese military. 

Japan is in denial about the environmental destruction caused by the Japanese military and industry. Dr. Tsuboi wants to shift some of the attention to the locals. He has tested water in the region and has found it is perpetually contaminated with per-and poly fluoroalkyl substances, better known as PFAS.  

Dr.  Tsuboi brought us to the assembly hangar of the Mitsubishi Heavy Industry in the airport's terminal building. The site is the location of the final Lockheed Martin  F-35 assembly plant in Japan. In a sense, this is the belly of the Asian beast. 

The US and Japan have a deal worth $23.1 Billion for the purchase of 105 advanced fifth generation nuclear-capable F-35 fighter jets. This totals $220 million per plane. Ask the Japanese people and they’ll tell you there are better ways to spend this money. The Japanese become irate when they learn that this entire operation results in their environment being contaminated and their health seriously compromised. 

There is a wave of indignation sweeping the country among increasing numbers who realize this militaristic bellicosity has an ugly side effect that may poison the unborn in future generations. 

To be continued to -2- from Tokyo 
 
Pat, Ken'ichi, and Rachel
