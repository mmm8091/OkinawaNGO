Financials

At Pacific Environment, transparency and accountability are core to how we operate. We are committed to stewarding our resources responsibly to maximize our impact across communities and ecosystems. Our financial practices reflect our values: investing in grassroots leadership, advancing bold climate solutions and sustaining long-term environmental justice.

Below, you’ll find our most recent IRS Form 990s and financial statements that demonstrate how donor and partner support drives meaningful change. We are proud to share how your trust fuels our mission.
