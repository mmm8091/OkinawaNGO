# US presence literature positioning v1

Status: `research_only / candidate_analysis / central_writeback=no / frontend_ready=no`

This package positions the Okinawa NGO database against scholarship on anti-base mobilization, garrison society and community relations, legitimation and attitudes, transnational legal/environmental accountability, and organizational-network methods.

Files:

- `bibliography_v1.csv`: 18 verified academic sources, with DOI/stable URL, methods, findings, and project relevance.
- `claim_positioning_v1.csv`: separates established scholarship, claims reproducible with current project data, candidate contributions, and missing-data conditions.
- `manifest.json`: package status, counts, boundaries, and file hashes.
- `../../docs/us_presence_literature_positioning_v1.md`: Chinese synthesis and research design.

Core boundary: observed service, donation, sponsorship, or community contact may support a claim about garrison reproduction or community mediation. It does not by itself demonstrate a legitimation effect. Likewise, zero direct cross-group organization edges in the bounded current inputs does not demonstrate that people, recipients, or informal ties are absent.

The package does not modify central data, the frontend, or `outputs/formal_comm_v3/`.
