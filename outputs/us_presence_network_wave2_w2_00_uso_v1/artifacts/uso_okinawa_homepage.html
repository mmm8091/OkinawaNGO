Thank you so much for this wonderful Birthday surprise you pulled off for my son. This deployment, he managed to miss: Thankgiving, Christmas, New Year 's, His Birthday and numerous Weddings and births from this very large family which he is a big part of. You have managed to make one young man very happy. Thank you again for all the good you do. A Marine that retired just about a month ago said something that really resonated with me. He said: "During my service, I wasn't home very much, but I was always with family." It is people like you and your organization that make these young men feel this way. May God Bless you & watch over you, so you may continue to bring joy to these men while they serve their country.
Joan Hollinger Find out how to support USO
