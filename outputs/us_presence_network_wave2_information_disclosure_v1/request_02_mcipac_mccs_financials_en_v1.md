## Ready-to-paste request

Dear FOIA Officer:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I request access to the following existing records concerning **Marine Corps Community Services Okinawa / MCIPAC MCCS**.

### Time period

For each retail fiscal year ending nearest January 31 in **2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025**. If the responsive local records are instead maintained by federal fiscal year, please use **FY2021 through FY2025**.

### Records requested

1. Annual audited financial statements, audit reports, or audit opinions for the MCCS Okinawa / MCIPAC MCCS installation nonappropriated-fund instrumentality.
2. The year-end installation-level balance sheet or statement of financial position; consolidated operating statement or profit-and-loss statement; cash-flow statement; and accompanying notes.
3. Existing annual budget-to-actual or operating-statement reports at the major cost-center, program-group, or installation level.
4. Existing schedules or reports that separately identify, where maintained, appropriated-fund support, nonappropriated-fund sales or revenue, sponsorship or donation revenue, central transfers, operating expenses, capital expenditures, and program expenses.
5. Existing allocation schedules or reports that disaggregate a Pacific, Japan, MCIPAC, or other regional total to Okinawa installations, programs, or cost centers.
6. Any chart-of-accounts legend, cost-center legend, report cover sheet, or transmittal needed to interpret the requested annual tables.

This request seeks existing reports and data and does not ask the Marine Corps to create a new financial statement, allocation, or calculation. Marine Corps MCCS policy describes a centralized financial system, standard cost centers, installation balance sheets, and profit-and-loss reports; those existing report types are intended to help identify the requested records.

If the local MCIPAC office maintains only installation reports and Headquarters Marine Corps, Manpower and Reserve Affairs, or the Business and Support Services Division maintains the consolidated statements or audit files, please refer the relevant portion to that custodian and notify me. If referral is not possible, please identify the likely custodian so that I may submit a separate request.

I request tabular records in their existing XLSX, CSV, or system-export format where practicable and narrative reports as searchable PDF. I do not seek point-of-sale transaction data, patron or beneficiary information, employee PII, bank-account information, internal-control vulnerabilities, vendor trade secrets, or installation-security information. Please redact protected information and release all reasonably segregable annual or aggregate portions. Please provide rolling releases where practicable.

If the scope requires clarification or narrowing, please contact me before closing the request. If five years is too burdensome, please process the most recent completed retail fiscal year first and contact me regarding a staged release of the remaining years.
