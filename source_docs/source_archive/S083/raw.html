Okinawa Youth Council Program: The U.S. Consulate General Naha of the U.S. Department of State announces an open competition for organizations to submit applications to carry out a program for the Okinawa Youth Council. The recipient organization will recruit, screen the participants (college and university students), and will design four-week program comprised of three weekly virtual webinars and a three-day in-person workshop in summer 2024 in Okinawa Prefecture.
