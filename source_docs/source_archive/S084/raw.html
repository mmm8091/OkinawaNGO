U.S. Mission to Japan

Okinawa Youth Council Program (Discontinued)

About this program (historical reference)

Historical funding: $5K – $10K per award

Prior eligibility: Eligible applicants: City or township governments; Public and State controlled institutions of higher education; Nonprofits having a 501(c)(3) status with the IRS, other than institutions of higher education; Nonprofits that do not have a 501(c)(3) status with the IRS, other than institutions of higher education; Private institutions of higher education; Individuals.

The U.S. Consulate General Naha of the U.S. Department of State announces an open competition for organizations to submit applications to carry out a program for the Okinawa Youth Council. The recipient organization will recruit, screen the participants (college and university students), and will design four-week program comprised of three weekly virtual webinars and a three-day in-person workshop in summer 2024 in Okinawa Prefecture. Funding Opportunity Number: NAHA-PAS-FY24-02-M001. Assistance Listing: 19.040. Funding Instrument: G. Category: O. Award Amount: $5K – $10K per award.
